Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

1. Information we collect

  • TikTok account identifiers: when you authorize the app, TikTok provides your Open ID and an access/refresh token, used solely to upload videos to your own account on your behalf.
  • Generated content: the story text and video files you generate are stored locally on your own server.

2. How we use information

Your tokens are used exclusively to authenticate with TikTok's Content Posting API and upload videos you asked the app to post. We do not sell, share, or disclose your information to third parties.

3. Text-to-speech note

Narrated audio is produced by a cloud text-to-speech service (Microsoft Edge's neural TTS), which receives the story text to generate speech. No other personal information is shared with this service.

4. Data retention and deletion

Tokens and generated videos are stored on your own server. You may delete videos at any time from the file server interface, and you can revoke the app's TikTok access at any time in your TikTok settings under "Manage app permissions".

5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app operator through the developer portal associated with this app.
