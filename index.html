Story Video Uploader

A personal tool that turns AI-written short stories into narrated, captioned videos — and posts them privately to your own TikTok account for review before anything goes public.

What it does

Story Video Uploader runs a fully local, automated video pipeline. It writes a short story with a local AI language model, narrates it with text-to-speech, renders the narration over gameplay-style background footage with word-synced captions, and then uploads the finished video to TikTok.

Every upload is created as a private (self-only) post on the authorizing user's own account. Nothing is ever posted to other accounts, and nothing goes public until the account owner reviews and publishes it themselves.

How it works

  1. Story generation. A local AI language model writes an original short story in a chosen genre (scary, funny, confession, and more).
  2. Narration. The story is converted to speech locally using neural text-to-speech, producing a natural voice-over with word-level timing.
  3. Video rendering. The narration is rendered over background footage (such as Minecraft parkour or subway visuals) with synchronized captions using ffmpeg.
  4. Private upload. The finished video is uploaded to the user's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API (Direct Post), with a title and hashtags, always as a private post.
  5. Human review. The user opens the TikTok app, reviews the private video, and decides whether to publish it.

Privacy & data handling

  • Single-user, personal use. This app is not a service. It has no accounts, no login, and no other users — it operates only on the owner's own TikTok account.
  • Minimal data. Only the OAuth tokens needed to post to the authorizing user's account are stored, on the owner's own machine. No data is sold, shared, or analyzed.
  • Private by default. Videos are posted as self-only (private). Publishing is always a separate, manual step by the account owner.
  • Revoke anytime. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's Settings & privacy → Security & permissions.

AI-generated content

All stories are original works generated by AI. Videos produced by this pipeline are labeled as AI-generated when published, in line with TikTok's AI-generated content policy.

FAQ

Who can use this app?

It is a personal tool built for its owner. There is no sign-up and no public service — authorization connects it to your own TikTok account only.

Does it post publicly without my approval?

No. Every video is posted privately (self-only). You review it in the TikTok app and publish it manually if you like it.

What permissions does it request?

Only user.info.basic (to identify your account) and video.publish (to upload videos on your behalf). Nothing else.
